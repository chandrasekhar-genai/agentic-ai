# Incident severity levels

Severity is set by the first responder and can be changed by the incident commander.

## SEV1

Customer-facing outage or data loss affecting many customers. Page the on-call engineer and incident commander immediately. Acknowledge within 15 minutes. Post a status page update within 30 minutes and then every 30 minutes until resolved.

## SEV2

Major degradation or a security alert that needs urgent review. Acknowledge within 30 minutes. Status page update only if customers are affected.

## SEV3

Minor degradation with a workaround. Handled during business hours. Acknowledge within 4 hours.

## SEV4

Cosmetic issues and questions. Tracked in the backlog.

## Post-incident review

Every SEV1 and SEV2 needs a blameless post-incident review within 5 business days. The review lists the timeline, root cause, and action items with owners and due dates.
